About

Hi, I'm Christiana.

A Salesforce & nCino expert who believes the best automation feels invisible — it just makes hard things easier for the people doing the work.

11 certifications. 48 superbadges. 5+ years across financial services & SaaS, turning "this should be easier" into solutions that actually are — and increasingly, doing it with AI in the loop.

I'm a certified Salesforce and nCino expert with deep, hands-on experience across both platforms. On the Salesforce side, that's Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Experience Cloud, Financial Services Cloud, CPQ, and NPSP — clean configuration, thoughtful flow design, and stakeholder collaboration. On the nCino side, that's the full commercial banking suite — fee & disbursement automation, discretion controls, borrowing structures, and the data model that ties it all together.

I've been in the Salesforce ecosystem for over 5 years — hands-on work spanning financial services and SaaS. That mixed-industry experience has shaped how I work: I've seen what scales, what breaks under audit, and what users actually adopt versus what looks good in a demo. Most recently, the last 15 months in SaaS have sharpened my Salesforce work across sales operations and customer experience, supporting users across multiple business units. I bring genuine dual fluency — when the right answer is a Salesforce flow, I build a Salesforce flow. When it's an nCino approval process, I build that. Most real problems need a bit of both.

I'm also leaning hard into AI. I hold the Salesforce AI Associate and Agentforce Specialist certifications, and I've built a multi-purpose AI admin agent (powered by Claude, connected to Salesforce, Slack, and Gmail through MCP) that takes recurring admin work off my plate while keeping me firmly in the loop on anything externally-visible. Responsible AI guardrails, prompt engineering, and named workflows are part of how I work now — not someday.

Earlier in my career I worked on a major implementation at Affinity Credit Union, including audit automation and CPQ configuration. I've also been responsible for end-to-end Salesforce admin work — security models, sharing rules, validation, deployment management — across multiple orgs.

Outside of paid work, I volunteer as the data administrator at Lofty Heights Christian Centre, a two-campus church in Calgary and Regina. I'm currently leading their NPSP Phase 1 implementation: custom objects, role hierarchies, sharing models, and a 1,185-row data migration. I also built and deployed a QR-based check-in system in Production — Apex, Lightning Web Components, and an Experience Cloud Site — picking up Apex along the way.

I'm always curious about better ways to do this work — happy to chat about Salesforce, nCino, NPSP, AI agents, or whatever you're trying to untangle.
