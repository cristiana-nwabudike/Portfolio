LHCC Salesforce NPSP Implementation

A full Salesforce NPSP build for a two-campus church — donated time, real outcomes.

The overview.

Lofty Heights Christian Centre — a two-campus church in Calgary and Regina — was running on Aplos and spreadsheets. They needed a real platform that could handle membership, attendance, ministry involvement, giving, and tax receipting across both campuses. I volunteered to lead the implementation end-to-end: scoping, build, data migration, and rollout.

The challenge.

  • Two campuses with different cultures, leadership, and operational needs — but one organization.
  • 1,185 rows of member data scattered across an Aplos export and a 2026 Google Sheet, with email as the only reliable match key.
  • Need for tight security so each campus only sees its own members, while leadership sees both.
  • Aggressive volunteer timeline — six months with paid work as the day job.

The solution.

Data model that fits the church

Built custom fields on Contact (Campus, Member Status, Membership Date, Last Attendance, Preferred Contact, First Visit Date, and more), Opportunity (Campus, Fund, Giving Method, Tax Receipt Sent), and custom objects for Attendance and Ministry Involvement — including formula fields for tenure tracking.

Seven-role security hierarchy

Designed and configured a role hierarchy (Church Leadership → Calgary/Regina Campus Admin → Ministry Leader → Greeter), Org-Wide Defaults set to Private, four criteria-based sharing rules using Campus, and three cloned profiles. Object permissions managed via Permission Sets at onboarding.

1,185-row data migration

Merged the Aplos export and 2026 Google Sheet into a MASTER sheet with email as the match key, deduped against Salesforce duplicate rules (set to Allow with Alert), and migrated using Salesforce Inspector — clean, traceable, and verifiable.

Twelve Campaign records for fund tracking

One Campaign per fund per campus, giving leadership real visibility into giving by fund and by site without manual reconciliation.

Comprehensive Jira backlog

Authored a full product backlog — 14 Epics, 57 user stories with acceptance criteria, mapped to 12 two-week sprints, 192 total story points. Everything trackable, prioritized, and ready to hand off.
