nCino Fee & Disbursement Automation

Automating disbursement creation and core-banking sync for commercial lending.

The overview.

Disbursement records in commercial lending used to be created and reconciled manually — a slow, error-prone process that pulled lending and operations teams away from higher-value work, and one that occasionally introduced data drift between nCino and the core banking system.

The challenge.

  • Manual creation of disbursement records from fee data, with no enforced link between the two.
  • Disbursement amounts being modified outside of fee records, causing reconciliation issues.
  • Third-party fees occasionally being waived in error, with no validation guardrail.
  • When fees were deleted, related disbursement records were left orphaned and aggregates went stale.

The solution.

nCino-integrated Salesforce flow

Designed a flow that automatically generates and calculates disbursement records from fee data and syncs them seamlessly with the core banking system, eliminating manual entry.

Validation rules with teeth

Created validation rules that prevent third-party fees from being waived and enforce that disbursement amounts can only be modified through their associated fee records — protecting compliance.

Cascade handling on delete

Configured automatic removal of associated disbursement records when a fee is deleted, with real-time recalculation of the loan's disbursement aggregate so data integrity is maintained without manual cleanup.
