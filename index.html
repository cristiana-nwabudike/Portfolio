About

Built for scale, designed for people.

I specialize in translating messy, manual business processes into clean Salesforce and nCino solutions that teams actually want to use.

On the Salesforce side, that's Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Experience Cloud, CPQ, and Financial Services Cloud — sales process optimization, case management, validation rules, flow automation, and the dashboards that drive real decisions. On the nCino side, that's deep commercial lending work: fee & disbursement automation, discretion controls, and borrowing structure design.

I'm also leaning hard into AI — building Agentforce-style solutions, MCP-connected admin agents, and prompt-engineered workflows that take recurring tasks off my plate without giving up oversight.

Outside of paid work, I volunteer as the data administrator for Lofty Heights Christian Centre, where I'm leading a full NPSP implementation across two campuses.
