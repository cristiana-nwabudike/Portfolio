My approach

Four habits that actually ship.

1 Understand first

Before touching a single field, I shadow the user. Most "requirements" only make sense once you've seen how the real work actually happens.

2 Declarative when possible

Flow, validation rules, and formula fields handle 90% of what people reach for Apex for. Configuration is faster to ship, safer to change, and easier to inherit.

3 Test in stages

Sandbox → UAT → production. I'd rather catch a broken flow with one user than discover it Monday morning across forty.

4 Document like a teammate

Every field has a real description. Every flow has a clear purpose comment. The next admin shouldn't have to reverse-engineer my work.
